Honors Focus Area Reflection

EGL focus area and how I plan to explore it

What operations means to me

When I think about operations I think about how a system actually runs from day to day. Operations is about how people resources processes and tools come together to produce an output in a way that is efficient and reliable. Engineering often focuses on technical pieces like software hardware or physical systems while operations makes sure those pieces fit together and keep working over time.

Even if an engineer designs a strong technical solution it can still fail if it is not planned and managed well. Understanding operations matters for any engineering major because it forces you to think about questions like efficiency reliability cost and scalability. It is not enough to ask if something can work. You also have to ask if it can work consistently for real users and real organizations.

What I hope to gain from Tauber

Through the Tauber Institute for Global Operations I want to learn how large organizations design and manage complex systems. I am especially interested in how operations scale across teams locations and time zones and how that shows up in everyday decisions.

I also want to see how technology data and operations connect to improve efficiency. I am curious about how data pipelines dashboards and software systems support large operational decisions and how engineers and business teams work together around that data. Overall I hope Tauber will give me direct exposure to industry problems and show me how engineering perspectives and business perspectives can meet in the middle.

How I ranked my focus areas

For my primary focus I chose Global Business and Operations. I am drawn to this area because I want to see how complex systems behave at scale and how engineering can make those systems more efficient and reliable.

I ranked Entrepreneurship as my second choice. I like the idea of building systems and solutions that create real impact. Entrepreneurship asks you to notice problems design solutions and think about how those solutions can grow. A lot of that work involves taking new technologies and turning them into something that brings value to people which is something I care about. I also know that creativity does not always come easily to me so choosing Entrepreneurship as a focus area would push me to think in new ways.

After those two I ranked Research third Design fourth and Public Service fifth. These are still important areas but right now I feel the strongest connection to operations and to building things that can eventually live in the real world.

How I will explore my focus areas

There are a few concrete steps I can take to keep exploring Global Business and Operations and Entrepreneurship.

  • Pursue internships or consulting projects that involve operational systems data infrastructure or business analytics so I can see how engineering work supports real organizations.
  • Take classes that focus on systems operations or entrepreneurship so I can better understand how technical decisions and business decisions affect each other.
  • Work on student projects where teams build solutions for real clients. These projects will help me practice both the technical work and the operational thinking that go into shipping something that people actually use.

By combining course work internships and project based work I hope to build a clearer picture of how I want to connect my engineering skills with global operations and eventually entrepreneurship.
