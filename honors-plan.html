Developing Proficient Exemplary
Communication X As an ENGR 101 Instructional Aide I spend a lot of time helping first year students with MATLAB and C++. Every student gets stuck in a slightly different way so I have to switch between walking through code line by line and stepping back to talk about the big idea. Being a Michigan Orientation Leader also pushed me to speak comfortably with students and families about academics and life at Michigan. I want to keep getting better at explaining technical work by presenting in my CS classes and mentoring students through IA or tutoring roles. I also plan to write clearer documentation for my projects and contribute to shared codebases so I can practice communicating technical ideas in writing, not just out loud.
Creativity X A lot of my work so far has involved finding creative ways to solve technical problems. At Dallas Tech I helped build dashboards that pulled data from several systems and turned it into something clients could understand at a glance. In my classes I have built things like a MapReduce framework and a forum post classifier where I had to experiment with different designs before something felt right. I plan to keep building side projects where I can try out new tools and ideas without worrying about grades. I also want to do more hackathons and team coding competitions where we have to come up with a working solution quickly. Taking advanced software and systems classes will give me more chances to design and compare different approaches.
Empathy X When I help ENGR 101 students in office hours I see a lot of frustration when code will not run. To actually help them I have to listen first and figure out what they are really confused about instead of just fixing the bug for them. Orientation work has also shown me how overwhelming the first weeks of college can feel and reminded me to be patient with people who are still finding their footing. I want to keep working in roles where I support other students who are learning hard material. Staying involved in programs that help new students transition into engineering will give me more practice slowing down listening and meeting people where they are. I also want to keep checking in with teammates about workload and stress instead of just focusing on the task.
Entrepreneurial Mindset X Most of my experience so far has been on the technical side rather than building full ventures. I have seen hints of an entrepreneurial mindset though especially when I helped develop a warehouse management system at BAPS Akshardham. There we looked at how inventory was really being tracked and built something that fit the actual problem better. I plan to take entrepreneurship or innovation focused courses at Michigan so I can learn more about turning technical ideas into products people will actually use. I also want to join startup style hackathons or student groups where teams tackle real problems for users. Those experiences will push me to think beyond the code and consider value impact and sustainability.
Ethics X In internships and projects I have worked on systems that handle financial and operational data which makes accuracy and reliability matter a lot. I have been part of testing and validation work to make sure the data is handled correctly and that the systems do what we say they do. Those experiences have made me more aware that bugs are not just technical issues they can affect real people and organizations. I want to keep learning about responsible software development through coursework and by paying attention to topics like data privacy and system reliability in my projects. As I work on larger systems I plan to ask more questions about who is affected by the tools we are building and how failure would show up for them. That mindset should help me make more thoughtful choices as an engineer.
Global/Cultural Awareness X Working as an Orientation Leader put me in front of students and families from many different backgrounds. Hearing their questions and concerns made it clear that people come to Michigan with very different experiences and expectations. Internship teams have also shown me that people from different cultures and roles approach problems in their own ways. I plan to study abroad during my time at Michigan so I can live and learn in another country and see how engineering is taught and practiced there. I also want to keep choosing classes and projects where I can work with teammates who bring different perspectives. The more I do that the better I will get at adjusting my communication and assumptions.
Grit/Resilience/Persistence X Computer science has already taught me that things rarely work the first time. Many of my projects only came together after long debugging sessions and a lot of small failures. Balancing classes internships and other commitments has also required me to keep going when my schedule felt packed. I want to keep pushing myself with challenging CS courses and projects that stretch my skills instead of only choosing the comfortable options. I also plan to look for internships where I can work on complex real world systems that take time to understand. Those experiences will keep building my ability to stay focused when problems are messy and do not have quick fixes.
Leadership X In ENGR 101 students often look to me for direction when they are stuck and I have to balance guiding them with letting them think for themselves. As an Orientation Leader I helped groups of new students navigate their first days on campus and tried to create an environment where they felt comfortable asking questions. These roles have shown me that leadership is as much about listening and setting a tone as it is about making decisions. I plan to take on more leadership responsibilities in group projects and student organizations where I can help a team move toward a shared goal. Continuing to mentor students in technical courses will help me practice leading by example and supporting others rather than just telling people what to do. Over time I want to become someone teams naturally trust in difficult moments.
Lifelong Learning X Computer science changes quickly and I like exploring new areas as they come up. Through classes internships and personal projects I have spent time with topics like distributed systems machine learning and data engineering. Being an IA also keeps me learning because explaining material to others forces me to really understand it myself. I plan to keep taking advanced CS courses and using side projects as a way to try out new tools and ideas. I also want to keep an eye on what technologies are being used in industry and learn the ones that fit my interests so I stay adaptable. Reading technical blogs documentation and open source code will help me keep learning outside the classroom.
Risk Management X Most of my risk related experience has come from testing and debugging. At Dallas Tech I helped build testing systems that caught errors before they reached production which made our systems more reliable. I have not yet had many chances to make big design decisions where tradeoffs and uncertainty are front and center. I want to work on larger technical systems where I need to think more carefully about reliability scalability and failure modes. In those settings I can learn how senior engineers weigh tradeoffs and decide which risks are acceptable. I also plan to bring more structured testing and code review habits into my own projects.
Systems Thinking X Many of my projects have required me to think about how different pieces fit together. When building data pipelines for example I have to understand how APIs databases and processing tools interact rather than only focusing on one component. Those experiences have helped me see that changing one part of a system often affects several others. I plan to take more systems oriented CS courses and choose projects where I am responsible for connecting multiple components instead of just one module. Drawing diagrams and documenting architectures will help me keep track of how everything is connected. Over time I want to get better at spotting bottlenecks and weak points in a design before they turn into problems.
Teamwork X During internships I worked closely with engineers DevOps teams and QA to build and ship software. Getting things done meant clear communication and being willing to adjust when someone else was blocked. As an Orientation Leader I also relied on other leaders to support incoming students and keep events running smoothly. I want to keep joining projects where different people own different parts of the work so I can practice coordinating across roles. Paying attention to how I share updates ask for help and support others will help me become a stronger teammate. I also hope to be someone who keeps the group focused and positive when projects get stressful.

SMART Goals

Continue growing as a leader by organizing impactful community and student initiatives. By the end of 2026 I will lead at least one large scale community event through BAPS that involves coordinating volunteers managing logistics and supporting community engagement. I will also mentor at least two younger students or members by helping them develop technical or leadership skills. I will measure success by the completion of the event and by maintaining regular mentorship meetings throughout the year.
